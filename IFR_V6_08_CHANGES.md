# V6.08 IFR Approach check-in

Accepts a position report such as 10 NW or 10NW in place of an altitude on Approach check-in. SoCal alone is accepted when the destination Approach facility is SoCal. Existing callsign, current arrival ATIS and approach-request checks remain required. Rejections now identify the actual missing required items in speech and feedback.

The exact call “SoCal, Cessna 172SP, 10 NW, inbound with Whiskey Palomar, ILS 24” passes at Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced with arrival information Whiskey and ILS runway 24 configured. Letter W and altitude-based alternatives also pass. Incorrect callsigns and ATIS codes are still rejected.

Local checks: targeted Approach checks at all levels, IFR stage-flow checks and a complete IFR flight through saved reports passed. Physical microphone/audio recognition was not tested. Custom-domain CNAME remains included.
