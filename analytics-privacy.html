Usage analytics privacy

FK ATC COM Trainer offers optional Google Analytics to help improve the training experience. Analytics runs automatically when a Google Analytics stream is connected, unless you have opted out or your browser sends a supported privacy signal. No analytics popup opens automatically. Open Analytics privacy at the bottom of the trainer to turn it off or back on. Choosing No thanks does not limit training.

What is measured

  • Visits, approximate visitor counts, browser and device information.
  • Which app sections and training stages are used, and whether training is started, completed or reset before completion.
  • Active time: time while the app is visible, excluding periods more than 60 seconds after the last interaction.
  • Approximate country, region/state and city derived by Google Analytics from the network connection. This is not GPS location and may be inaccurate.

Optional profile and usage system

When the separate usage service is connected, a random guest browser ID, approximate country/region/city, last activity date and active usage seconds are stored for the trainer owner. Choosing Google sign-in associates your Google display name with this usage profile and joins the current guest usage to it. Email is used only transiently to authorize an owner account; it is not stored in the usage database or sent to Google Analytics. Open Profile and usage to sign out or delete your usage data. Signed-in sessions expire after one day (owner access after one hour); guest credentials expire after 90 days. Inactive guest profiles are removed after 180 days. Usage batches are removed after two days, while accumulated totals remain. Signed-in profiles remain until deleted. Browser privacy signals and the Analytics privacy choice apply to both tracking systems.

What the trainer does not send

The analytics integration does not send callsigns, names, email addresses, audio, typed radio transmissions, flight routes, selected airports, or flight report contents. It does not request precise location, assign an account ID or use advertising personalization.

During short network interruptions, up to 200 usage events may be held temporarily in this page's memory for up to 30 minutes, then sent when the connection returns. Disabling analytics discards this temporary queue.

Cookies and choices

When enabled, Google Analytics uses first-party cookies to distinguish browsers and sessions. They are configured to expire after 180 days without extending their expiry on each visit. Visitor counts are approximate: one person on multiple devices may count more than once.

Your preference is saved on this browser. Select Analytics privacy at the bottom of the trainer to change it. Declining stops future analytics and removes this integration's Google Analytics cookies. Previously sent data is not recalled. Browser Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals keep this integration off.

Who processes the information

Google Analytics processes usage data and IP-based approximate location. The trainer owner views aggregated reports in their Google Analytics account. IP addresses are not placed in the trainer's custom event payloads or flight reports.
