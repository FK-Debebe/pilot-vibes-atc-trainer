V6.28 — IFR radar-vector instructions

Departure assigns an explicit simulated heading instead of “proceed radar vectors.” The initial training vector uses the selected departure runway heading. This is a simulated vector, not a published airport procedure or a clearance for actual flight.

Departure and Enroute readbacks require the assigned heading, altitude and aircraft callsign. Numeric and spoken digits are accepted. Missing or wrong headings cannot be replaced with “radar vectors.” Enroute retains the assigned heading. Initial IFR clearance routing may still describe “radar vectors, then direct ...”.

Validation passed: radar-vector checks at all three training levels, full IFR flight, IFR stage flow, and scripted departure-procedure regression.
