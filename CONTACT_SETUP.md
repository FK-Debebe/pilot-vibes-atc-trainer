# Direct contact email delivery — V6.22

Contact us posts Name, Email, Message and trainer version to https://formsubmit.co/fwdskillzone@gmail.com. No email application is needed. FormSubmit handles spam verification and delivery, then redirects to the included contact-thanks.html page. Only the entered form fields and fixed metadata are posted; analytics never receives message text or sender email.

## One-time owner activation

1. Upload all V6.22 public app files, including contact.js and contact-thanks.html, to GitHub Pages. Preserve CNAME. Cloudflare changes are not needed.
2. Open the published Contact us page, enter your own name/email and a short test message, and select Submit.
3. Complete FormSubmit verification if prompted.
4. Check fwdskillzone@gmail.com, including Spam, for the FormSubmit activation email and confirm the form. Delivery is not ready until confirmation is complete.
5. Submit another test and confirm it reaches the inbox. A service acceptance or thank-you page does not prove inbox delivery; check Spam as needed.

No activation email was triggered during local tests. No live delivery has been verified. FormSubmit is a third-party delivery service; its availability and filters affect submission. Native HTTPS POST preserves the service's verification/error pages and also works without contact.js. Do not disable its CAPTCHA. The contact form does not use the Cloudflare usage database.
